Preparation and expectations

Eligibility and general requirements

Requirements will vary by position. Not every role will require a university degree.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be at least 18 years old on the date of application.
  • Applicants must meet the educational, professional and technical requirements of the relevant position.
  • Relevant qualifications, certifications and experience may be required depending on the role.

Professional readiness

  • Strong communication and teamwork skills may be expected.
  • Professional conduct, responsibility and integrity are important.
  • Technical applicants may be asked to demonstrate relevant skills.
  • Leadership applicants may need relevant management experience.
  • Applicants must provide accurate information and genuine qualifications.

A proposed future process

Recruitment process

The following stages describe a possible process. Passing any stage does not guarantee selection.

  1. 01

    Official Vacancy Announcement

    Approved opportunities, if available, would be announced officially.

  2. 02

    Sub-Company and Position Selection

    Applicants would identify the company and approved position of interest.

  3. 03

    Online Application Submission

    A submission process may be provided only after recruitment officially opens.

  4. 04

    Application Review

    Submitted information would be reviewed against the published criteria.

  5. 05

    Eligibility and Qualification Assessment

    Relevant eligibility, qualifications and experience would be assessed.

  6. 06

    Technical or Professional Assessment

    An assessment may be used where it is relevant to the position.

  7. 07

    Interview Process

    Shortlisted applicants may be invited to one or more interviews.

  8. 08

    Final Selection

    Final decisions would follow the approved selection process.

  9. 09

    Official Offer and Appointment

    Any offer would be made through official channels and subject to approval.

  10. 10

    Onboarding and Orientation

    Selected candidates may receive role-specific onboarding.
