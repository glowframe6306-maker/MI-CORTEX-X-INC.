Privacy Policy

MI CORTEX X INC. is committed to handling personal information responsibly and only for legitimate business and service-related purposes.

Information collected through inquiries, newsletter subscriptions, and application forms may be used to respond to customer questions, manage service requests, review applications, and improve public communication.

This website uses privacy-sensitive practices and should be designed to include consent notices, secure form handling, and unsubscribe mechanisms for newsletter subscribers.

Local Careers prototype

The Careers workflow collects an applicant’s name, email address, optional phone number, selected company and position, application statement, age/privacy confirmations, and optional PDF CV/supporting documents solely for recruitment review. Applications remain disabled unless both the server-side opening-date and official-approval gates are enabled. The page describes future recruitment plans, not current vacancies.

In the local prototype, applicant details and PDF contents are encrypted before storage in a local SQLite database. In a configured serverless deployment, applicant details and document metadata are encrypted before storage in Neon PostgreSQL, while PDFs are stored in a private Vercel Blob store and are available only to an authenticated Careers administrator. Neon and Vercel process the relevant data to provide those services. Up to three PDFs of 10 MB each are accepted; files are not malware-scanned. The configured retention period defaults to 90 days and is limited to 30–365 days; authorized administrators can delete applications sooner, and expired records and files are removed by scheduled cleanup. Local and serverless encryption keys must be backed up securely; losing the applicable key makes encrypted records unreadable.

Do not enable recruitment or collect real applicant information until the company has separately approved the deployment, provider configuration, retention period, and all required privacy disclosures and safeguards.

These prototype terms do not authorize real recruitment or replace approved recruitment privacy terms. Any real application service requires a reviewed privacy notice, approved recruitment process, secure hosting, malware scanning, access controls, retention policy, and data-protection review before collection begins.

Important note

Data collection, retention, and use must reflect the website’s actual practices and must remain consistent with the company’s verified service scope. The local Careers prototype is disabled until both its server-side date gate and an explicit official-approval setting are enabled.
